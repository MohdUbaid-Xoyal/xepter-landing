## Xepter, Wired Into Every GoHighLevel Sub-Account

We connect Xepter to your GoHighLevel location or agency account, configure conversation settings, and make sure SMS, MMS, voice, and WhatsApp all show up correctly inside the conversations you already use. For agencies, we help standardize the setup across every client sub-account so onboarding a new client takes minutes, not hours.

If you're moving from another messaging tool inside GoHighLevel, we help migrate your number, contacts, and active workflows so nothing gets lost in the switch.

![Team configuring a GoHighLevel integration](/images/ns-img-33.jpg)

Once connected, your team keeps working inside GoHighLevel exactly as before — messaging just works, on every channel.

### What This Service Delivers

- Full GoHighLevel connection setup for one location or an entire agency account
- Standardized sub-account rollout for agencies onboarding multiple clients
- Migration support for numbers, contacts, and workflows from another tool

### Who It Is For

- Agencies running client accounts on GoHighLevel
- Teams switching from another messaging tool inside GoHighLevel
- Businesses setting up GoHighLevel messaging for the first time
