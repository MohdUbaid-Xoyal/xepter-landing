## Every Channel, Synced to the HubSpot Timeline You Already Use

We connect Xepter to your HubSpot portal so every SMS, MMS, call, and WhatsApp message logs directly to the contact record — no separate tool for your sales or support team to check. We configure the integration, test delivery and webhook events, and make sure your existing HubSpot workflows can trigger and respond to messages.

If you're consolidating from a separate texting or calling tool, we help migrate your number and message history so your team doesn't lose context.

![Team configuring a HubSpot integration](/images/ns-img-33.jpg)

The result is one contact timeline in HubSpot that actually reflects every conversation, on every channel.

### What This Service Delivers

- Full HubSpot portal connection with contact-level message and call logging
- Webhook and workflow configuration for automated messaging triggers
- Migration support for numbers and message history from another tool

### Who It Is For

- Sales and support teams running on HubSpot
- Businesses consolidating messaging tools into one CRM
- Teams setting up HubSpot messaging for the first time
