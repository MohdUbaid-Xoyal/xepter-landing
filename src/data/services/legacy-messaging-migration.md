## Switch Providers Without Losing Your Conversation History

Moving off another SMS or voice provider is often delayed because of one fear: losing history, breaking active conversations, or dropping calls during the cutover. We plan the migration around your number porting timeline, coordinate the cutover window, and help import contacts and message history where your previous provider supports export.

We also review your existing automations and workflows so equivalent triggers are rebuilt in Xepter before you switch, not after.

![Team planning a messaging platform migration](/images/ns-img-33.jpg)

By the time your number ports over, your team is already working in Xepter — with the history and workflows they need to pick up right where they left off.

### What This Service Delivers

- A migration plan built around your number porting timeline
- Contact and message history import where your previous provider allows it
- Workflow review to rebuild equivalent automations before cutover

### Who It Is For

- Businesses switching from another SMS or voice provider
- Teams consolidating multiple messaging tools into one platform
- Agencies migrating client accounts from a legacy system
