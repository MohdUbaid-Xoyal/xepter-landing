## Give Every Team the Access They Need, Nothing More

As your team grows, so does the risk of the wrong person sending the wrong message — or losing track of who can access what. We help you design a role structure that fits how your organization actually works: who can send campaigns, who can manage numbers and compliance, and who just needs to see conversation history.

We configure roles and permissions across your organization, document the setup, and help you adjust it as your team changes.

![Team reviewing access and permissions setup](/images/ns-img-33.jpg)

The result is a team that can move fast, with access boundaries that hold up under audit.

### What This Service Delivers

- A role structure designed around how your team actually operates
- Full configuration of roles and permissions across your organization
- Documentation to support onboarding new team members going forward

### Who It Is For

- Growing teams adding messaging and voice access for the first time
- Businesses that need clear audit trails for compliance
- Agencies managing access across internal teams and client accounts
